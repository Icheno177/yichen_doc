# Evidence Rules

## 1. Label the statement before using it

| Label | Meaning | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FACT | Externally verifiable statement | Cite source and date. |
| INTERNAL DATA | Authorized company record or measurement | Name owner, period, extraction method, and limitation. |
| ASSUMPTION | Input not established as a fact | State rationale, range, and what would invalidate it. |
| CALCULATION | Reproducible transformation of inputs | Show formula, units, and rounding. |
| JUDGMENT | Interpretation that joins facts/assumptions | Explain the bridge and alternatives considered. |
| RECOMMENDATION | Proposed decision or action | Assign owner, timeline, resource, KPI, and stop/go trigger. |

Never silently convert one label into another.

## 2. Evidence levels

| Level | Typical sources | Allowed use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Regulators, government, company formal announcements and financial reports, SEC/exchange filings, courts/administrators, formal technical documentation, primary databases | Critical facts, legal/financial status, official product behavior. |
| B | Peer-reviewed research, universities/institutions, high-quality technical papers, reputable industry databases | Critical analytical support and validated methods. |
| C | Broker research, consulting, professional research, authoritative media | Context, market trends, cross-checking; do not make it sole proof of a critical fact. |
| D | Company PR, marketing, interviews | The issuer's own claim, explicitly attributed. |
| E | General media, blogs, forums, Zhihu, Reddit, social media | Lead generation and sentiment only; never a critical claim. |

Critical factual claims require A or B. Use C for trend context. Quote D as a claim by its issuer. Use E only to identify a question worth verifying.

## 3. Evidence ledger

Maintain a compact ledger while researching:

| Claim | Label | Source / level / date | Direct support | Limits or contradiction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Record missing primary evidence as a finding, not an invitation to fill the gap with narrative.

## 4. Inference-bridge checks

Reject or qualify these shortcuts unless the intermediary chain is evidenced:

- Degree → decision capability.
- Registered capital → founder personal wealth.
- Financing → viable or profitable business.
- User count → revenue or profit.
- Benchmark score → enterprise ROI.
- Technology capability → business value.
- Large market → company market share.
- Revenue growth → quality of growth.
- Cash-flow improvement → durable fundamentals.
- Correlation → causation.

Ask: what mechanism, comparator, denominator, period, and alternative explanation would have to hold? Then collect evidence for that bridge or downgrade the claim.
