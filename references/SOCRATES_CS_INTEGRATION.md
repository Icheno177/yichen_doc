# Socrates.CS Integration

## Role boundary

Socrates.CS is an optional falsification and critical-analysis system. It is not required to invoke or complete yichen_doc and is **not enabled by default**.

- **yichen_doc:** frames the decision, gathers and grades evidence, calculates, writes the document, and specifies action.
- **Socrates.CS:** challenges causal chains, assumptions, counterevidence, confidence, and recommendation robustness.

The ordinary yichen_doc evidence-quality review is separate from Socrates.CS. Do not invoke Socrates.CS, route work to it, or treat a manual check as a substitute Socrates.CS run unless the user explicitly requests that workflow. Do not claim a Socrates.CS review occurred when it did not.

## When to use it

Use only after an explicit user request naming Socrates.CS, a falsification framework, or an equivalent critical-analysis workflow. Topic suitability—including investment, budget allocation, causality, strategy, or AI business value—is not permission to invoke it. When requested, use it after a draft has a decision question, evidence ledger, calculations, and recommendation.

## Handoff packet

Provide only what is needed:

1. Decision question and alternatives.
2. Pivotal claims with labels and evidence levels.
3. Assumptions, formulas, and scenario results.
4. Material risks and the proposed recommendation.
5. The specific challenge requested: causal bridge, counterevidence, scenario break, or recommendation reversal.

## Questions to ask

- What evidence would falsify the leading judgment?
- Which inference is being made from proxy to outcome?
- What alternative explanation fits the same data?
- Which assumption has the highest recommendation sensitivity?
- What must be true for the stop/go rule to be credible?

Integrate confirmed challenges as explicit changes to evidence, assumptions, risks, or the final decision. Keep unconfirmed objections visible as open questions.
