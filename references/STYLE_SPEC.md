# Style Specification

## Tone

Write in a direct, concise, professional style. Lead with the answer and its confidence boundary. Prefer concrete nouns, dated facts, quantities, and named conditions over abstraction.

Use Chinese report conventions when writing Chinese: a precise title, one-sentence conclusion, clear evidence labels, and short action-oriented recommendations.

## Sentence rules

- Prefer “2025Q2 revenue grew 18% year over year, driven by X; margin fell 3pp because Y” over “growth momentum was strong.”
- Name the actor, action, time, denominator, and unit where material.
- Keep facts, assumptions, calculations, and judgments in distinct sentences or labels.
- Use conditional language only for actual uncertainty: “If monthly retained volume stays below 10,000 for two months, stop expansion.”
- State what is unknown and why it matters.

## Tables and charts

Use a table for comparisons, evidence ledgers, scenarios, risks, or ownership. A table needs a decision-relevant title, unit/date, and source note.

Use a chart only when it reveals a trend, distribution, trade-off, or scenario difference more clearly than a table. Do not use decorative charts. Label axes, units, period, and source; keep assumptions in the note.

## Recommendation form

Write recommendations in this form:

> **Action:** … | **Owner:** … | **By:** … | **Resource:** … | **KPI:** … | **Stop/go:** …
