# Example: Industrial Inspection Software Segment

## Decision

**Question:** Should Company A enter the domestic mid-market visual-inspection software segment in 2027?
**Answer:** Run a paid design-partner validation in one electronics subsegment; do not fund broad sales expansion until integration time and defect-reduction value are measured.

## Evidence and boundary

| Statement | Label | Evidence | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The target workflow requires image review and escalation. | FACT | Hypothetical customer interviews, Level D | Issuer/customer claims need contract-stage verification. |
| Annual serviceable sites are 1,200–1,800. | ASSUMPTION | Bottom-up account list | List excludes unobserved plants. |
| Addressable annual software revenue is ¥36m–¥108m. | CALCULATION | sites × ¥30k–¥60k ARR | Price and adoption are unvalidated. |

## Decision logic

TAM language would not change the entry decision. The decision turns on whether Company A can deploy within 30 days and reduce manual review time without unacceptable false negatives.

## Action

**Action:** Sign three paid design partners. **Owner:** GM, Industrial. **By:** 2027-03-31. **Resource:** ≤¥1.2m. **KPI:** ≥20% review-time reduction and no critical defect escape in the agreed test set. **Stop/go:** Stop broad expansion if median deployment exceeds 30 days or two partners fail the defect threshold.
