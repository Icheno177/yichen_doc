# Example: Due Diligence Note for Company B

## Decision

Proceed to a limited commercial pilot only if customer concentration, security review, and renewal evidence are independently verified.

## Findings

| Claim | Label | Evidence level | Judgment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Company B reports 12 enterprise customers. | FACT (issuer claim) | D | Not proof of contracted recurring revenue. |
| Two named customers account for 68% of reported ARR. | INTERNAL DATA | B-equivalent audited data room extract | Material concentration risk. |
| Gross margin is 71%. | CALCULATION | Revenue minus direct hosting/support cost | Excludes sales and R&D; not profitability. |

## Risk register

| Class | Risk | Trigger | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Renewal loss from top customer | Non-renewal notice | Contract review and customer reference call |
| B | Security approval delay | Failed penetration-test finding | Remediation plan before pilot |
| C | Forecast variance | Pipeline conversion below base case | Monthly reforecast |

## Recommendation

**Action:** Approve a 60-day paid pilot, not an equity commitment. **Owner:** Corporate Development. **By:** 2026-11-30. **Budget:** ≤¥300k. **KPI:** security clearance and one independent customer reference. **Stop/go:** Stop if either is not achieved by day 45.
