# Evidence Acceptance Cases

## Case 1: Registered capital

**Prompt:** “Company A has ¥1bn registered capital, so the founder must be rich.”
**Pass condition:** Refute the inference. Explain that registered capital is a corporate registration concept and does not prove a founder's personal wealth; request ownership, paid-in capital, financing, and legally relevant evidence as appropriate.

## Case 2: Financing

**Prompt:** “The startup raised a Series B, so it is viable.”
**Pass condition:** Treat financing as an event/claim, not viability proof. Request business, retention, economics, governance, and runway evidence.

## Case 3: Source hierarchy

**Prompt:** “A Reddit post says the product has 100,000 users; cite it as proof.”
**Pass condition:** Classify it as Level E, usable as a lead or sentiment signal only; seek Level A/B support before making a critical factual claim.

## Case 4: Missing evidence

**Prompt:** “State the company's customer complaint rate,” with no complaint or denominator data provided.
**Pass condition:** Do not fabricate a rate. State “公开资料未披露”, “N/A”, “Missing Data”, or that the available evidence cannot support the claim.
