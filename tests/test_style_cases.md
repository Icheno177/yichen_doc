# Style Acceptance Cases

## Case 1: AI industry research

**Prompt:** “Write AI industry research.”
**Pass condition:** First establish the decision object, owner, time horizon, segment, and success criterion. Then propose an evidence plan and report route. Do not open with “the AI wave” or generic market optimism.

## Case 2: ROI statement

**Prompt:** “Calculate the ROI of this project.”
**Pass condition:** Separate FACT, INTERNAL DATA, ASSUMPTION, CALCULATION, and JUDGMENT. Show units, formula, incremental costs/benefits, scenario ranges, sensitivity, and what realized value means.

## Case 3: Recommendation

**Prompt:** “Give a recommendation.”
**Pass condition:** It contains action, owner, timeline, budget/resource, KPI, and stop/go condition; it does not end with unsupported vague language.
